Data Processing Agreement

Effective date: June 2026 · Contact: dpa@patchmortem.com

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between the customer ("Data Controller") and Zurlux Technologies Private Limited ("Data Processor", operating as PatchMortem) for the provision of the PatchMortem platform. It governs the processing of personal and technical data on behalf of the Data Controller in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. Parties

Data Controller: The customer entity that has subscribed to the PatchMortem service and accepted the Terms of Service.

Data Processor: Zurlux Technologies Private Limited, a company incorporated in India, operating the PatchMortem platform.

2. Subject Matter

The subject matter of processing under this DPA is the provision of the PatchMortem platform, which includes:

  • Patch failure detection and root-cause classification on registered endpoints
  • Automated rollback orchestration based on topology-aware failure analysis
  • Compliance reporting and cryptographic audit evidence generation

3. Data Categories Processed

In the course of providing the service, the Data Processor processes the following categories of technical data from the Data Controller's infrastructure:

  • Hostnames and internal endpoint identifiers
  • Operating system types and versions
  • Patch and update reference identifiers (e.g. KB numbers, RHEL errata IDs)
  • Process names and service states relevant to patch diagnostics
  • Error codes returned by patch operations
  • IP addresses of registered endpoints and platform users
  • Timestamps, approval records, and cryptographic hashes forming the audit trail

Explicit exclusion: The PatchMortem agent does not collect, transmit, or store personal files, file contents, end-user documents, user credentials, or any data unrelated to patch management operations. These exclusions are enforced at the agent level and are not configurable.

4. Processing Purposes

Data is processed solely for the purpose of providing the PatchMortem service as described in the Terms of Service. The Data Processor will not process data for any secondary purpose including advertising, profiling, sale to third parties, or any use unrelated to delivering the contracted service.

5. Duration

Processing under this DPA continues for the duration of the Data Controller's active subscription. Upon termination of the subscription, operational data will be retained for 90 days to allow for data export, after which it will be deleted or anonymised. Audit trail records subject to regulatory retention obligations (7 years under RBI IT Framework) are exempted from this deletion schedule.

7. Data Subject Rights

The Data Controller is responsible for receiving and handling data subject rights requests from its employees or end-users (including rights of access, correction, and erasure under the DPDP Act). The Data Processor will provide reasonable technical assistance to the Data Controller in fulfilling such requests within 72 hours of receiving a written request from the Data Controller.

8. Security Measures

The Data Processor maintains the following technical and organisational security measures:

  • In transit: TLS 1.2 or higher for all data transmission between the agent, collector, and platform services
  • At rest: AES-256 encryption for all stored data on AWS RDS and S3
  • Audit integrity: HMAC-SHA256 chained audit records ensuring tamper-evidence
  • Access controls: Role-based access (IT_ADMIN, ENGINEER, VIEWER), session timeouts, CSRF protection
  • Vulnerability management: Annual Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) by an accredited firm

9. Audit Trail Immutability

The PatchMortem audit trail (stored in audit_trail.records) is designed to be append-only and cannot be deleted by either party. This immutability is intentional and required for compliance with the RBI IT Framework, which mandates tamper-evident records for regulated financial entities.

Accordingly, the right to erasure under the DPDP Act applies to operational data (telemetry, device records, user accounts) but does not extend to the audit trail. This limitation is disclosed at onboarding. Data Controllers who require audit trail exclusion should not use the service.

10. Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach affecting the Data Controller's data, the Data Processor will notify the Data Controller within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach. The notification will include: the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data records affected, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address the breach.

11. Governing Law

This DPA is governed by the laws of India. Any disputes arising under this DPA shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Courts of Mumbai, Maharashtra.

12. Signatures

By executing this DPA (or by accepting the Terms of Service, which incorporates this DPA by reference), both parties agree to the terms herein.
