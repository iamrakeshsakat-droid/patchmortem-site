Know WHY it failed before you fix it

A 4-stage pipeline — extraction, vector match, LLM fallback, risk overlay — matches every failure against 215 known patterns across Windows, RHEL, Ubuntu, Kubernetes, VMware ESXi, SQL Server, and Oracle. Your team sees the root cause at 0.95+ confidence before the rollback starts.

  • 4-stage classifier: extraction → vector match → LLM fallback → risk overlay
  • 215 patterns across Windows, RHEL, Ubuntu, K8s, ESXi, SQL Server, Oracle
  • Confidence score shown to approvers before rollback

Tamper-evident proof for your RBI audit

Every remediation decision, approval, and rollback is written to an append-only, HMAC-SHA256 cryptographically chained audit log — designed specifically for RBI IT Framework Section 4.2 evidence requirements. Tamper with one record and the chain breaks.

  • HMAC-SHA256 with key versioning
  • Append-only records — built for RBI IT Framework Section 4.2
  • One-click compliance export for RBI, PCI-DSS, SEBI, IRDAI
