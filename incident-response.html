Incident Response Policy

Effective date: June 2026 · Contact: security@patchmortem.com

1. Purpose

This policy describes how Zurlux Technologies Private Limited ("PatchMortem") detects, responds to, and communicates security incidents that affect the PatchMortem platform and its customers. Our goal is to minimise impact, restore service quickly, and communicate transparently throughout the incident lifecycle.

2. Scope

This policy covers all incidents affecting the PatchMortem platform, including:

  • Data breaches — unauthorised access to, or disclosure of, customer data
  • Service outages — complete or partial unavailability of the platform
  • Security vulnerabilities — confirmed vulnerabilities in the platform or its dependencies
  • Unauthorised access — any access to customer tenants or internal systems not authorised by the account holder

3. Incident Classification

Severity Definition Examples
P0 — Critical Data breach or confirmed unauthorised access to customer data Exfiltration of audit records, credential compromise, tenant data exposed
P1 — High Complete service outage lasting more than 1 hour API unavailable, database unreachable, all agent heartbeats failing
P2 — Medium Degraded performance or partial feature unavailability Slow response times, notification delivery delayed, reporting unavailable
P3 — Low Minor issues with minimal customer impact UI display issues, non-critical background job failures

4. Response Timelines

Severity Acknowledgement Customer Notification Resolution Target
P0 Immediate Within 72 hours of awareness Best effort, continuous
P1 Within 4 hours Status page update within 4 hours Within 8 hours
P2 Within 8 hours Status page update within 8 hours Within 48 hours
P3 Next business day At resolution Next sprint cycle

5. Notification Process

Affected customers will be notified through the following channels:

  • Registered email — notification sent to the primary contact address on the account
  • In-app banner — persistent banner visible on the PatchMortem dashboard for active incidents
  • Status page — real-time updates at status.patchmortem.com

For P0 incidents involving personal data, notification will comply with obligations under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

6. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are expected to:

  • Maintain accurate and up-to-date contact information in the PatchMortem dashboard
  • Designate at least one IT contact for incident communication
  • Respond promptly to PatchMortem requests for information during active incidents affecting their infrastructure
  • Report suspected security incidents or vulnerabilities to security@patchmortem.com

7. Post-Incident Review

For P0 and P1 incidents, PatchMortem will publish a root cause analysis (RCA) within 14 calendar days of incident resolution. The RCA will include: a timeline of events, the root cause, remediation steps taken, and preventive measures implemented. RCAs are shared directly with affected customers and published to the status page.

8. Contact

To report a security vulnerability: security@patchmortem.com

To enquire about an ongoing incident: incidents@patchmortem.com
