What the RBI IT Framework 2023 requires

The five patch management mandates

The RBI IT Framework 2023 (successor to the 2011 IT Policy guidelines) explicitly mandates the following for all regulated banks and NBFCs.

📋
Documented patch management process
Regulated entities must have a documented, board-approved patch management policy covering identification, testing, deployment, and verification of patches across all IT assets.
🧪
Evidence of patch testing before deployment
Every patch must be tested in a non-production environment before deployment to production. Testing outcomes must be documented and available for audit inspection.
🔗
Audit trail of all patch activities
A complete, tamper-evident record of every patch activity — who applied it, when, on which asset, and what the outcome was — must be maintained and available for at least 7 years.
🚨
Incident response for patch failures
Failed patches must trigger a documented incident response process with defined escalation paths, rollback procedures, and root cause analysis.
📊
Board-level reporting on IT risk
The Board or IT Strategy Committee must receive regular reports on patch compliance status, outstanding vulnerabilities, and remediation timelines.

Audit readiness

What your auditor will ask for — and where to find it

These are the five most common questions RBI IT auditors ask about patch management. Here is exactly how PatchMortem answers each one.

"Show me your patch management policy and approval records."
Every rollback and remediation action requires explicit four-eyes approval. The approver name, timestamp, IP address, and decision rationale are recorded in the cryptographic audit chain. Export the full audit log from the Audit Trail page in under 60 seconds.
Audit Trail → Export
"How do you test patches before production deployment?"
PatchMortem's predictive engine classifies every patch before deployment using a 5-stage AI pipeline — extracting signals, matching against a failure taxonomy, and scoring risk. The classification and confidence score are attached to every patch record as evidence of pre-deployment assessment.
Risk Predict → Assessment Report
"Prove your audit trail has not been tampered with."
Every audit record is signed with HMAC-SHA256 and chained to the previous record's hash — identical to blockchain integrity verification. Any modification to any record breaks the chain and triggers a TAMPER_DETECTED alert. Run Chain Integrity Verification from the Audit Trail page at any time.
Audit Trail → Verify Chain Integrity
"What is your mean time to detect and remediate patch failures?"
PatchMortem measures MTTD (time from patch application to failure detection) and MTTR (time from detection to completed rollback) from the audit trail in real time. These metrics appear on the SLA dashboard with historical trends and breach history.
SLA → MTTD / MTTR Dashboard
"What compliance frameworks do you currently satisfy?"
PatchMortem generates one-click compliance reports for PCI DSS 6.3 (patch management), HIPAA 164.312(b) (audit controls), SOC 2 CC7.2 (change management), and RBI IT Framework 2023. Each report includes a compliance score, control-by-control evidence, and an executive summary suitable for board presentation.
Compliance → Generate Report
